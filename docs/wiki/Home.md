# Welcome to the Doki wiki

Doki carries your game saves between devices, keeps every version in a history, and never replaces your current save until you choose to.

Play on your PC, pick up on your handheld, and come back again. Progress from another device waits for you to review it. You can **use it**, **keep it alongside** your own, or **keep your save**. Whatever you choose, the other version stays in your history.

> Doki is **pre-release**. Keep copies of your saves while you try it.
